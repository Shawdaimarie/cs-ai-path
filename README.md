# cs-ai-path

**A structured, self-paced path from computer science fundamentals to modern AI.**
Free resources. Clear order. No noise.

---

## What this is

Most learning roadmaps are link dumps: fifty resources per topic and no sense of which one to open first. This is the opposite.

Every tier below has:

- **One goal** — what you can do when you finish it
- **3–5 vetted resources** — not thirty
- **A checkpoint** — a concrete exercise that proves you're ready to move on
- **A time estimate** — realistic hours for a focused learner

Follow the tiers in order. Skip nothing unless you can pass the checkpoint cold.

## Who it's for

- Self-taught learners who want a university-grade sequence without the tuition
- Career changers moving into software or AI
- Engineers who learned to code but skipped the foundations and feel it
- Anyone who wants to understand how modern AI systems actually work, not just call an API

You need: a laptop, roughly 8–10 hours a week, and patience. No prior experience assumed.

**Total: ~720 hours.** At 10 hours a week that is about 18 months. At 20 hours a week, 9 months. The full sequence with rationale and checkpoints is in [CURRICULUM.md](CURRICULUM.md).

## How to use it

1. **Start at Tier 00**, even if it looks easy. The checkpoint will tell you if you can skip.
2. **Do the checkpoint before moving on.** Reading is not learning. Building is.
3. **Ship the capstone** for each tier to your own GitHub before starting the next one.
4. **Track your progress** by forking this repo and checking boxes in `CURRICULUM.md`.

## Principles

- **Order over volume.** The sequence is the product. Resources are interchangeable; the structure isn't.
- **Free first.** Every core resource is free. Paid alternatives are marked as optional.
- **Fundamentals before frameworks.** You will write a neural network in plain NumPy before you touch PyTorch.
- **Nothing is sacred.** If a better resource appears, we replace the old one. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Contributing
